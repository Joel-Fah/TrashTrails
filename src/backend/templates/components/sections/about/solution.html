{# Problem Definition #}

We didn't want to build just another app that sits on your phone. We wanted to build a movement.

TrashTrails is a crowdsourced mapping tool designed to turn every frustrated citizen into a Community Guardian. Instead of just being annoyed by a dump site, you snap a photo.

  • The "Proof" Factor: Your report is geolocated and mapped publicly. It’s no longer just "some trash somewhere"—it's a data point that can’t be ignored.
  • The AI Brain: We don’t just see a pile of "dorty." We use Computer Vision to look closer. Our models scan your photos to find recyclables (plastics, metals, glass) that are currently making up a huge chunk of our 600,000 annual tons of plastic waste.
  • The Leaderboard: Let’s make impact a sport. Every report earns you points. We’re gamifying the cleanup because, let’s be honest, a little competition makes the hard work of civic duty a lot more fun.
